Policies
Privacy Policy

At Springbrook Golf Course (IL), we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide while using our online tee time booking system.

Information Collection

  • Name: To personalize your account and tee time bookings.
  • Phone Number: To communicate regarding your bookings and provide updates.
  • Email Address: To send booking confirmations, updates, and relevant information.

Information Usage

  • Account management and tee time booking confirmation.
  • Notifications and updates about your bookings.
  • Marketing communications with your explicit consent — opt out any time.

Data Protection

  • Industry-standard HTTPS encryption for all data transmission.
  • Access restricted to authorized personnel only.
  • Data retained only as long as necessary or required by law.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell or trade your personal information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Terms & Conditions

Springbrook Golf Course (IL) offers customers the opportunity to engage in conversations and marketing communications via SMS.

Campaigns

  • Customer Care: Tee time reminders, alerts, and support via text. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Marketing: Special offers and events via text. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Stop and Help

  • Text STOP to opt out of any campaign.
  • Text HELP for assistance.

Carrier Disclaimer

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
